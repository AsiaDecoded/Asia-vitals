Seasonally adjusted series

The Ministry of Data and Statistics (Statistics Korea until October 2025) publishes no seasonally adjusted consumer prices, and the Bank of Korea’s ECOS database carries none either. Its monthly release reports unadjusted month-on-month changes only. This dashboard does not seasonally adjust any series itself, so it has no momentum chart.

Year-on-year rates

ECOS publishes index levels to two decimals. The y/y rates here are calculated from those indices and rounded to one decimal, which is how the ministry derives its published rates; they are checked against the published rates on every update.

Contributions and weights

The ministry does not publish a contributions series, so contributions are calculated here from the published indices and the published 2022 basket weights.

Weights

  • Published: ECOS gives the ministry’s 2022 basket weight (per 1,000) for every item, division and special aggregate. They are shown as % of CPI.

Contributions to y/y

  • Parts: agricultural, livestock and fishery products; industrial products excluding petroleum; petroleum products; electricity, gas and water; and services. These five make up all items.
  • Formula: the CPI is a Lowe index. Each part’s coefficient is its weight divided by its 2022 average index, scaled so that the weighted parts equal the all-items index in December 2022, when the 2022 basket was linked in. A contribution is coefficient × (index now − index a year earlier) ÷ all-items index a year earlier. The weighted items reproduce the published all-items index to within 0.01 index points in every month since December 2022.
  • Period: December 2023 on, the months whose year-earlier month is also on the 2022 basket. ECOS does not publish the weights of the earlier 2020 basket.
  • Rounding: weights are published to one decimal, so the parts add up to the all-items rate within about 0.02pp.

Energy

The ministry defines energy as nine items (release appendix: petroleum products, electricity, city gas and district heating) and excludes them from its core measure, but publishes no energy index. The energy index here weights the nine published item indices by their published 2022 weights (75.8 per 1,000) in the same Lowe form as the contributions, set to 100 in December 2022, so its y/y starts in December 2023. Food (142.0), energy (75.8) and the published core measure (782.2) add up to 1,000. Check: the same method applied to the six petroleum items reproduces the published petroleum products y/y within 0.1pp in every month.

Biggest contributors

Item contributions use the same method with each item’s published weight and index. The table ranks items by their average contribution over the latest three or six months.

CPI diffusion index

The diffusion index measures how broad-based inflation is: not how fast prices are rising on average, but how many of the things people buy are getting more expensive.

Definition

For each month, every individual item in Korea’s CPI is classified by its year-on-year price change: rising (above zero), falling (below zero) or unchanged. The chart shows the share of items rising, the share falling, and the diffusion index: share rising minus share falling, from −100 to +100.

Data

  • Source: the item indices in ECOS table 901Y009, with y/y calculated from the index.
  • Items: the 458 items of the current basket, January 2016 on. Category totals are excluded, so nothing is counted twice. y/y is rounded to one decimal, as published, before classifying.

Why the number of items varies

ECOS carries only the items in today’s basket. 443 of them have a y/y rate from 2016, 444 from 2020 and all 458 from January 2021, when items introduced with the 2020 basket gained a year of history.

Choices and limits

  • Unweighted: each item counts once, whatever its weight. The weighted picture is in the CPI itself and the contributions chart.
  • Threshold of zero: an item rising 0.1% counts the same as one rising 20%.
