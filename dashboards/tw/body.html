Consumer and producer prices

Taiwan’s consumer price index (CPI) is compiled monthly by the Price Statistics Section of DGBAS. It covers the goods and services bought by households nationwide (excluding Kinmen and Lienchiang counties), priced in 9 cities and 8 counties: 7 groups, 40 subgroups, 62 small groups and 368 priced items. Fresh vegetables, fruits and fish are priced three times a month, other items once.

What DGBAS publishes

  • CPI: index (2021 = 100), m/m and y/y for all items (linked series from 1959), the groups and subgroups (from 1981) and the 368 items (from 2013); goods and services with their subgroups; DGBAS core (excluding fruit, vegetables and energy), energy and other special groups; purchase-frequency groups; income groups; elderly households.
  • Seasonally adjusted: DGBAS seasonally adjusts the CPI (from 1959) and core CPI (from 2008) and publishes the adjusted index and m/m.
  • Weights and contributions: each release (table 1) gives the year’s weights of the groups and main subgroups and DGBAS’s contributions to the m/m, y/y and year-to-date change; table 14 gives every priced item’s weight. The page shows weights as % of CPI.
  • Producer and trade prices: PPI (2021 = 100, from January 2021, 2024 weights; it replaced the wholesale price index), domestic sales prices, and import and export prices in New Taiwan dollars and US dollars by HS section and end use.

Weights and base

The CPI is a Laspeyres-type index chained each December. Since the 2016 base, DGBAS updates the weights every year from household consumption two years earlier (2026 weights: 2024 structure; 2023 used 2019–2021 and 2024 used late 2022 to late 2023, because of the pandemic). Fresh vegetables and fruits have monthly weights (three-year moving average of monthly sales). The classification and items are reviewed every five years; the current base, 2021 = 100, started with the January 2023 index. DGBAS rescales earlier years to each new base and publishes the linked series. Because the fruit and vegetable weights change by month, the published group weights reproduce the headline index to about 0.05 index points, not exactly.

Asia tab: food

The Asia tab compares food and non-alcoholic beverages (COICOP 01) across economies. DGBAS does not publish that series: its food group includes alcoholic beverages and food away from home. The Asia tab’s Taiwan food series is calculated: DGBAS’s food index less alcoholic beverages (4 items) and food away from home (12 items, including coffee and drinks bought out, kindergarten snacks and school lunches), removed at DGBAS’s published weights (food and food away from home from release table 1, alcohol from its items in table 14; each year’s weights, linked at December, as DGBAS aggregates). Tobacco is not in DGBAS’s food group. Index December 2022 = 100, y/y from January 2024. Checks: the kept and removed parts recombine to DGBAS’s food index (gap under 0.001 index points) and their weights sum to the food weight; taking the month’s food weight from table 14 instead of table 1 changes y/y by at most 0.05pp (limit 0.06). The food series on this page is DGBAS’s own.

Core 1

Core 1 is CPI excluding food and non-alcoholic beverages (COICOP 01, as above) and energy, the core measure on the Asia tab. DGBAS does not publish it. It is calculated from DGBAS’s all-items index by removing the COICOP 01 food series and energy (gas, electricity, and fuels and lubricants, DGBAS’s energy boundary), each at DGBAS’s published weights for the year (food from release table 1, energy from its items in table 14: 3.7% of CPI in 2026, which matches 100 minus DGBAS core, vegetables and fruit), linked at December as DGBAS aggregates. Core 1 covers 82.0% of the 2026 basket. Index December 2022 = 100, y/y from January 2024. Checks: the parts recombine to the all-items index (gap under 0.001 index points) and their weights sum to 100; y/y moves by at most 0.02pp if the month’s food total from table 14 replaces table 1, and by at most 0.03pp if energy is removed as DGBAS’s energy index rather than its three subgroups (limits 0.06pp). Core 1 keeps food away from home, alcohol and tobacco.

Releases

DGBAS releases the CPI, PPI and import and export prices together at 16:00 Taipei time (16:00 Singapore) on the fifth working day after the month. The latest three months may be revised. Contributions and weights on this page are as first published.

Data

DGBAS Macro Database (nstatdb.dgbas.gov.tw), price tables; DGBAS’s linked CPI file for the all-items index before 1981; monthly releases on stat.gov.tw (tables 1 and 14) for weights and contributions. Built by the AsiaDecoded/asia-vitals pipeline (data/tw/cpi, data/tw/ppi), which fetches only on DGBAS release days.
