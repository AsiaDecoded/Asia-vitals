GDP

NSO compiles quarterly GDP by the production approach under the 2008 System of National Accounts, and publishes it with its socio-economic press conference in the month after each quarter, as an estimate for the latest quarter and a preliminary figure for the quarter before. Expenditure-side growth (final consumption, accumulation, exports and imports) appears only as rates in the release text, not in the tables.

What NSO publishes each quarter

  • GDP at current prices: VND billion and % of GDP, for the quarter and the year to date, by 28 rows: total; agriculture, forestry and fishing (with agriculture, forestry, fishing); industry and construction (mining, manufacturing, electricity, water supply, construction); services (14 ISIC sections, G to T); taxes on products less subsidies.
  • GDP at constant prices: the same rows in VND billion, with growth against the same period a year earlier.
  • Industrial production index (IIP): monthly and quarterly, against the same period a year earlier, for industry, its four sections and the VSIC two-digit divisions, including the 24 manufacturing divisions. This is the deepest official breakdown of manufacturing; the GDP tables show manufacturing as one row.
  • Annual (statistical yearbook database): GDP by sector at current prices from 1986 and at constant 2010 prices from 2005; GDP by expenditure (final consumption of households and government, gross capital formation, trade balance) at current prices from 1995 and constant 2010 prices from 2005.
  • Not published: seasonally adjusted GDP, quarter-on-quarter growth, a GDP deflator, nominal growth rates (Chart 1’s nominal growth is calculated here), quarterly expenditure tables (growth rates appear in the release text), contributions to growth as data (the release text gives each sector’s share of value-added growth), value added by manufacturing industry.

Bases and breaks

Constant prices are 2010 prices up to the fourth quarter of 2025 and 2020 prices from the first quarter of 2026 (“Gross domestic product at constant prices was calculated using the base year 2020”, NSO, second-quarter 2026 release). Current-price levels continue on the same scale (the 2026 change concerns constant prices only). An earlier revaluation, published in 2021, raised the level of GDP for 2011–2020; the 2020 quarterly report tables are on the old scale, the yearbook on the new. NSO’s report tables do not restate earlier quarters on the new basis, so levels across the change are not comparable; this page does not link them.

Data

NSO’s quarterly socio-economic report tables (“Biểu”, tables 1 and 2 and the quarterly IIP table), first quarter of 2020 to date, and the archived report files from 2010 to 2019 for real growth history (1994 prices to 2012), the latest published figure for each period; NSO’s PX-Web yearbook database (tables E03.02, E03.03, E03.08, E03.09) for annual figures. Built by the AsiaDecoded/asia-vitals pipeline (data/vn/gdp), which reads the report workbooks on NSO release days.

Consumer and producer prices

Vietnam’s consumer price index (CPI) is compiled monthly by the National Statistics Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Finance, formerly the General Statistics Office. The 2024 basket, in use from the November 2025 index, has 775 representative goods and services in 11 groups, weighted from the 2024 household living standards survey (about 62,000 households, four rounds). Earlier bases: 2019 (754 items, used for 2020 to October 2025), 2014 (654), 2009 (572), 2005 (494), 2000 (396), 1995 (300). NSO computes a geometric-mean Laspeyres index with fixed base-year weights (as stated for the 2014 basket).

What NSO publishes

  • CPI: all items, 11 groups, cereals, foodstuff, eating out, health services and education services; national, urban and rural; against the base year, the same month a year earlier, December of the previous year, the previous month, the quarter average and the year-to-date average. Regions and ten provinces against the previous month.
  • Core inflation: y/y, m/m and year-to-date average y/y, as % changes. NSO’s definition excludes food and fresh foodstuff, energy and State-managed goods including health and education services.
  • Gold and US dollar price indices: published with the CPI, not part of it.
  • Producer and trade prices: quarterly PPI for agriculture, forestry and fishing, industry and services with their sub-sectors; input prices for production; transport prices; export and import prices in US dollars; terms of trade. Annual figures in the statistical yearbook.
  • Not published: seasonally adjusted series, goods and services, energy, CPI excluding food and energy, food and non-alcoholic beverages, contributions to CPI (only some groups’ impact, in the release text), an all-economy PPI.

The 2014 weights are from NSO’s 2019 press release on the 2015–2020 method; the 2024 weights from its January 2026 press release on the 2025–2030 method, read off the infographic in which NSO published them. The 2019-basket weights (2020 to October 2025) were not found in any NSO table.

Bases and breaks

The base-year index is on 2019 = 100 from January 2020 to October 2025 in the monthly releases and on 2024 = 100 from November 2025; the yearbook database restates all of 2025 on 2024 = 100. NSO links its y/y and other rates across the change. This page does not chain the two bases into one index.

Releases

NSO releases the CPI on the 3rd of the following month, weekends included, around 09:00 Hanoi time (10:00 Singapore), with its socio-economic press conference (Decree 13/2026, from the April 2026 index). Until July 2024 the CPI came out on the 29th of the month itself; from August 2024 to March 2026, on the 6th of the following month. Quarterly producer and trade prices come with the CPI in quarter-end months.

Data

NSO monthly CPI workbooks (www.nso.gov.vn, from December 2021), NSO’s PX-Web yearbook database (pxweb.nso.gov.vn) for the history, NSO’s socio-economic report tables for quarterly prices, and the IMF CPI dataset for the long headline series. Built by the AsiaDecoded/asia-vitals pipeline (data/vn/cpi, data/vn/ppi), which fetches only on NSO release days.
