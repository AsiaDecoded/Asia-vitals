Consumer price index

The Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) compiles the CPI monthly on a 2010 = 100 base, classified by MCOICOP into 13 divisions (the COICOP 2018 structure, including insurance and financial services). The index starts in January 1980 (annual from 1960), y/y in January 1981.

Core CPI

DOSM’s core CPI excludes items with volatile prices (fresh food) and items with government-administered prices. It is published from January 2018 (y/y from January 2019), for the total and every division except alcoholic beverages and tobacco, all of whose prices are administered. It is DOSM’s own definition, not CPI excluding food and energy as used for cross-country comparison.

Producer prices

The PPI for local production (2010 = 100) measures prices received by producers at the first stage of commercialisation, excluding indirect taxes and separately invoiced transport. DOSM publishes the index, y/y and m/m for the total and five sections, and a seasonally adjusted index and m/m for the total, agriculture and mining from January 2017, water supply from January 2022, and electricity and gas from January 2017 to December 2024 only. There is none for manufacturing, and no seasonally adjusted y/y.

Seasonal adjustment

DOSM publishes no seasonally adjusted CPI, so this page has no seasonally adjusted CPI m/m. The PPI m/m in chart 3 and the tile is DOSM’s own, from its seasonally adjusted index.

Releases and revisions

DOSM releases the CPI around the 19th to 23rd of the following month, and the PPI around the 28th of the month after next, at 12:00 Malaysia time. DOSM may revise the latest three months of the PPI.

Data

All figures are as published by DOSM, none is calculated. OpenDOSM data catalogue (open.dosm.gov.my): cpi_headline, cpi_headline_inflation, cpi_core, cpi_core_inflation, cpi_annual_inflation, ppi and ppi_1d. Built by the AsiaDecoded/asia-vitals pipeline (data/my/cpi/my_cpi.csv, data/my/ppi/my_ppi.csv), which checks for new data on DOSM’s release days.

Exchange rate

Ringgit per US dollar, daily, from the BIS bilateral exchange rate series (WS_XRU), compiled from national sources, as published; full history from 1971. A higher number means a weaker ringgit.
