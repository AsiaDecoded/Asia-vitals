GDP

  • Source: the Office of the National Economic and Social Development Council (NESDC), Quarterly Gross Domestic Product, statistical tables (Excel), as published: GDP at current market prices (Table 3), chain volume measures with reference year 2002 (Table 4) and their y/y growth (Table 4.1, one decimal). Quarters from Q1 1993, y/y from Q1 1994; full years from 1993. NESDC marks preliminary years (p1) and revised quarters (r). Released about seven weeks after the quarter, at 09:30 Bangkok time.
  • Sectors: NESDC’s own grouping: agriculture (ISIC A); industrial (B to E: mining, manufacturing, electricity and gas, water and waste); services (F to U), which in NESDC’s tables include construction.
  • Chain volume measures are not additive: sectors do not sum to GDP in real terms. At current prices they do.
  • Calculated here (flagged in the tooltips): nominal GDP growth (Chart 3, Nominal), each quarter’s current-price level on the same quarter a year earlier; and the year to date (open circles on Charts 5 and 6), the chain volume levels of the quarters published so far on the same quarters a year earlier. NESDC’s annual chain volume level is the sum of its four quarters, so this is its own annual method; for the first half of 2026 it gives 2.4%, the figure in NESDC’s release.
  • US dollar levels (Charts 1 and 2): World Bank, World Development Indicators, as published: GDP and GDP per capita in current US$ and in constant 2015 US$; GNI per capita on the Atlas method, the series behind the World Bank’s income groups. The dashed line marks 1987, the year Thailand became lower-middle income (data year). CAGR: compound annual growth from 1987 to the latest World Bank year, calculated on this page.
  • Annual growth, World Bank (Chart 5): WDI real GDP growth (NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG) from 1961; from 1994 it matches NESDC’s annual figures.
Source: NESDC; World Bank, WDIAsia Decoded

Data

Every price figure on this page is published by Thailand’s Trade Policy and Strategy Office (TPSO), Ministry of Commerce, and taken from its Open API (index-api.tpso.go.th). Nothing is calculated, linked or seasonally adjusted here.

Consumer prices

  • Index: general CPI, 2023 = 100. The 2023 basket (464 items) has been used since January 2025. TPSO links the 2023-based index back to January 1976; the page shows that series as published.
  • Classification: TPSO’s own eight groups (food and non-alcoholic beverages; apparel and footwear; housing and furnishing; medical and personal care; transport and communication; recreation, reading, education and religion; tobacco and alcoholic beverages; and non-food as a whole), not COICOP. TPSO’s Thai series names are shown in English translation.
  • Year-on-year rates: TPSO’s published rates for every month.
  • Weights: TPSO’s published expenditure shares for the 2023 basket, % of CPI, from its rebasing note (February 2025). TPSO publishes weights for the groups, core, fresh food and energy only, so sub-groups carry none.
  • Areas: Bangkok and vicinity, and the Central, North, Northeast and South regions, all items, as published. Low-income households: TPSO’s separate CPI for low-income households, 2023 = 100.

Core CPI and other aggregates

TPSO’s core CPI excludes fresh food and energy: 126 of the 464 items, 30.3% of the basket. TPSO also publishes CPI excluding food, non-alcoholic beverages and energy, which removes all food rather than fresh food only and is closer to the measure most countries call core; the page shows it for comparison across countries. Both are TPSO’s series, as published.

Seasonal adjustment

TPSO publishes no seasonally adjusted CPI, so the page has no seasonally adjusted month-on-month measure.

Producer, export and import prices

  • PPI: TPSO Producer Price Index, 2021 = 100, by product (all items from January 2015, y/y from January 2016) and by stage of processing (from January 1995). Released with the CPI.
  • Export and import prices: TPSO indices in baht, 2012 = 100, from January 2000, released around the 28th of the following month.

Not on this page

TPSO publishes no goods and services split, no contributions to y/y in its database and no diffusion measure, and no weights below group level, so the page has none of these.

Source: Trade Policy and Strategy Office, Ministry of CommerceAsia Decoded

Exchange rate

Baht per US dollar, daily, from the BIS bilateral exchange rate series (WS_XRU), compiled from national sources, as published; full history from 1981. A higher number means a weaker baht.
