Seasonally adjusted series

SingStat publishes seasonally adjusted indices and m/m changes for all items, all items less accommodation, all items less imputed rentals, food and every division and group of the CPI. It publishes none for the MAS core measure or its services, retail and other goods, and electricity and gas components. This dashboard does not seasonally adjust any series itself, so MAS core appears in y/y terms only, and the momentum table and m/m charts use SingStat’s own adjusted series.

Contributions and weights

SingStat does not publish contributions to CPI inflation, so they are calculated here.

Weights

  • Published: item, group, division and series weights are SingStat’s published 2024 weights (Rebasing of the CPI, 2024 as base year, Information Paper IP-E61), for all 157 items, summing to 10,000. CPI less accommodation, CPI less imputed rentals, MAS core, energy and CPI less food and energy are sums and differences of published weights. CPI less food and energy also uses the published 2014 and 2019 weights (IP-E48 and IP-E61).
  • MAS core parts: MAS publishes the weights of food, services, retail and other goods, and electricity and gas (MAS–MTI Consumer Price Developments, Table A.1): 2019 basket 21.10, 31.73, 11.11 and 1.88; 2024 basket 20.42, 31.73, 10.25 and 1.98 (% of CPI). MAS published none for the 2014 basket. For it, food (21.67) and electricity and gas (2.49) are SingStat’s published weights, and the remaining 41.48 of MAS core is split into services 29.39 and retail and other goods 12.09 in the ratio implied by the published MAS core indices over 2014–18. The same calculation reproduces MAS’s published 2019 and 2024 splits; adding up SingStat’s 2014 item weights by MAS’s classification gives 29.22 and 12.26. Food is split at SingStat’s published weights into food excl. food and beverage serving services and food and beverage serving services: 7.74 and 13.93 (2014), 6.82 and 14.28 (2019), 6.51 and 13.91 (2024).

Contributions to y/y

  • CPI: the first chart splits CPI y/y into MAS core, accommodation and private transport, which make up all items. Weights are SingStat’s published accommodation and private transport weights for each basket (2014: 22.87 and 11.49; 2019: 21.98 and 12.21; 2024: 26.56 and 9.06, % of CPI); MAS core is the remainder.
  • Formula: within a basket, each index is set to 1 in the month before the basket starts (December 2013, 2018 and 2023). A part’s contribution is its weight × the change in its rebased index over the year × the all-items index in that month before the basket ÷ the all-items index a year earlier. For the twelve months that straddle a change of basket, the year is split at that month: old weights before, new after.
  • Rounding: because published weights are rounded and apply to the basket’s reference prices, the parts add up to the CPI rate within 0.02pp on average and 0.1pp at most (January 2024).
  • Food and energy: a third chart splits CPI y/y into CPI less food and energy, food excl. FBSS, and energy (electricity, gas, petrol, other fuel and lubricants) at SingStat’s published weights for each basket, by the same method; the energy series start in January 2015, so it begins in January 2016. The parts add up to the CPI rate.
  • CPI less food and energy: a chart splits its y/y into the three parts it shares with MAS core (services, retail and other goods, FBSS) and the two it adds (accommodation, private transport ex-fuel), with the same weights as the other contribution charts (services and retail and other goods: MAS; 2014 estimated). The line is the weighted sum of these parts, chained; it is within 0.13pp of the published-weights CPI less food and energy series, 0.04pp on average.
  • MAS core: the second chart splits MAS core y/y into food excl. food and beverage serving services, food and beverage serving services, services, retail and other goods, and electricity and gas, at MAS’s published weights (each part’s share of MAS core), by the same formula with the MAS core index in place of all items. It starts in January 2015; for 2015–2019 the services and retail and other goods weights are estimates (above). On the 2014 basket the indices are set to 1 in January 2014, when SingStat’s services, retail and other goods, and electricity and gas series begin. The parts add up to the MAS core rate within 0.02pp on average and 0.07pp at most.

Biggest contributors

Item contributions use the published 2024 item weights and the published item indices, for months whose year-earlier month is also on the 2024 basket (January 2025 on). The table ranks items by their average contribution over the latest three or six months.

Goods and services

The goods and services charts split all items in two. Services are MAS core’s services, accommodation, FBSS and private transport services (74.7% of CPI on the 2024 basket); goods are everything else (25.3%: food excl. FBSS, retail and other goods, electricity and gas, cars, motorcycles and fuel). Both are weighted indices on published weights for each basket, chained from January 2015 like CPI less food and energy, and goods are all items less services, so the two contributions add up to CPI inflation. Private transport services enter the 2024 basket only (see Services below); before 2024 they sit in goods.

Services

The services chart covers services broadly, as in the goods and services charts: MAS core’s services (31.7% of CPI), plus accommodation (26.6%: imputed rentals, actual rentals, housing maintenance and repairs), FBSS (13.9%) and private transport services (2.3%: maintenance and repair, parking, ERP and other services, vehicle insurance), 74.7% of CPI in all. It shows health services (outpatient and inpatient care, other health services, health insurance), land transport services (bus and train, point-to-point, commuting fares), airfares, actual rentals and other services (the rest, including imputed rentals, FBSS, education, telecoms, recreation and household services). Each is a weighted index on published weights for each basket (2014, 2019, 2024), chained like CPI less food and energy, from January 2015. For the 2014 basket, services are estimated, health and transport on the older classification, and actual rentals implied by the indices (SingStat does not split accommodation). SingStat indexes private transport services only from January 2024, so they enter the 2024 basket only, as private transport less cars, motorcycles and fuel (this carries bicycles and parts, 0.2% of CPI).

CPI diffusion index

The diffusion index measures how broad-based inflation is: not how fast prices are rising on average, but how many of the things people buy are getting more expensive.

Definition

For each month, every individual item in Singapore’s CPI is classified by its published year-on-year price change: rising (above zero), falling (below zero) or unchanged. The chart shows the share of items rising, the share falling, and the diffusion index: share rising minus share falling, from −100 to +100.

Data

  • Source: SingStat’s y/y rates for every CPI series (table M213781), January 2016 on.
  • Items: the lowest level of the classification with a published y/y in that month. Category totals are excluded, so nothing is counted twice.

Why the number of items varies

SingStat’s detailed item series start at different dates. 117 items have y/y rates from 2016, 118 from 2020, and the full 157 from January 2025, when items introduced with the 2024 basket gained a year of history. Some of the level shift at those dates reflects the change in coverage.

Choices and limits

  • Unweighted: each item counts once, whatever its weight. The weighted picture is in the CPI itself and the contributions chart.
  • Threshold of zero: an item rising 0.1% counts the same as one rising 20%.
